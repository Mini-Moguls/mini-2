Why Mini Moguls Market Day

CREATE. SELL. LEARN. Where little ideas become real skills

Mini Moguls Market Day is more than a market—it is a practical learning playground where young people discover what it takes to turn imagination into something customers value.

Parent & Partner Guide

  • Creativity in Action

    Young creators design, make and package real products with pride and originality.

  • Financial Awareness

    Participants learn simple costing, fair pricing, counting change and goal setting.

  • Confident Communication

    Welcoming visitors, explaining ideas and thanking customers builds lasting poise.

  • Supportive Community

    Parents, educators and Harare businesses cheering on the next generation of founders.
