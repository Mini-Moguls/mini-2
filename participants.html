About Mini Moguls Market Day

Who Mini Moguls Market Day is built for

Mini Moguls Market Day brings together three age groups of young entrepreneurs, alongside parents, schools, sponsors and the Harare community on Saturday, 12 December 2026.

Junior Moguls

Early Creators & Playful Sellers

Designed for our youngest participants taking their very first step into creating crafts, treats and greeting customers with close parent support.

Focus
Confidence, counting & creativity

Focus

Age-range confirmation notice

To keep all event communications transparent: the promotional poster lists ages 6–18, while the event concept note outlines three age categories covering ages 5–18 (Junior Moguls 5–8, Young Moguls 9–13, Senior Moguls 14–18). Parents of 5-year-olds are encouraged to confirm directly with the organising team when registering.
